How FRIS works

Eight stages, from upload to verified report.

Hash and extract

SHA-256 of the file, then a scan of readable strings for component names and versions.

Match CVEs

Components are checked against NVD records, with an offline set for demos.

Three agents

Threat intelligence, patch verification and risk analysis each produce their own evidence-based summary.

Score and anchor

A documented formula sets the risk score, then the report hash is chained into the ledger.

FRIS finds known issues in components it can identify. It cannot prove firmware is safe or find unknown vulnerabilities.
